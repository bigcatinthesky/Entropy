# Entropy
## Overview 
Entropy is a hard science fiction space exploration RPG. Featuring a wide range of vehicles, locations, and activities across the stranded Terminus system. You play as Elise, a cargo pilot stuck on a decaying station years after the connection between Terminus and the Solar system was severed. When new home is raised, you must undertake a journey to the system's furthest edges and deepest depths. Find those responsible, and follow the traces of someone you lost long ago.
## Gameplay
+ ### Arm, outfit, and upgrade your vessel for a variety of roles.
+ ### Acquire more ships to expand and specialize your fleet in addition to crew members to operate them.
+ ### Manage onboard systems and resources to keep your ship running and crew breathing on distant journeys.
+ ### Complete contracts to develop reputation with factions and unlock powerful equipment. 
+ ### Manage relationships with your inner circle to ensure maximum synergy and effectiveness with your crew.
+ ### Influence a decade long power struggle between super powers.
+ ### Navigate the emergence of secrets long thought, and better left, in the past.
+ ### Explore the truth behind your journey and the stranding of Terminus.
## World
### Over a decade after the severing from Sol, the Terminus system exists at the brink of collapse. 
+ The Colonization Oversight Administration originally tasked with developing the system has devolved into a brutally authoritarian society based only on profit and survival of the fittest. Though even its endless resources are not enough to stop the inevitable decay of industry.

+ The Administration remains in a tense cold war with the Workers Collective since the first rebellion shortly after the gate collapse. Despite its efforts, the Collective is still a flawed, often corrupt, democracy, struggling to feed its people and secure its territory with limited resources.

+ Rouges, scavengers, pirates, and smaller factions erode at the thinning grasp of the superpowers. They maintain control of contested or undefended territories and claim them as their own, some seek to ally with larger powers for security, some maintain neutrality, and others outright aggression.

+ Ships that stray too far into the outer system frequently go missing. Only sometimes to be found empty years later. It is rumoured something old lurks far beyond the five worlds, that unseen forces are at play, but those are just rumors.
